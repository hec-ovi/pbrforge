- [Cast](cast.png), [prompt](prompts/cast.md): fine neutral cement and distributed pores.
- [Weathered](weathered.png), [prompt](prompts/weathered.md): worn paste with dark mineral fines.
- [Graphite](graphite.png), [prompt](prompts/graphite.md): charcoal pigmented concrete.
