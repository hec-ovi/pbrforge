# glyph-atlas

Letter sheet, one lit glyph per cell. File: `src/gen/pattern/GlyphAtlas.ts`. Recipe: letter-atlas batch.

8 x 6 cells, charset in CONTRACT. `line` core width, `bevel` halo. Colors: core, halo, plate. Emission carries the lit glyph; basecolor is the unlit tube on a dark plate. Exact 4:3 sheet.
