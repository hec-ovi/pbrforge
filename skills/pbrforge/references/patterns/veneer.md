# Architectural veneer

`veneer` is a continuous fine directional wood surface, without plank joints,
large knots or baked lighting. `axis` follows the long grain. Periodic warped
fibres provide 3–15 mm grain detail under a separate satin varnish response.
`depth` controls shallow metric pore relief; `sheen` controls varnish variation.
The two colors are closely related wood tones, not alternating boards.

The shipped smoked walnut remains 1024² over 2×2 m (512 pixels/metre), preserving
the existing runtime UV contract. Use it for selected furniture faces, inserts,
and correctly oriented slats. Structural frames, appliance enclosures and broad
corporate wall fields use the separate alloy, composite and stone families.
