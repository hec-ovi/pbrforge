# grille

One condenser face. File: `src/gen/pattern/Grille.ts`. Recipe: `batch/cyberpunk/ac-unit.json`.

Geometry in metres from the face centre: `r`, `theta`. `split` is grille diameter as a fraction of the face. `line` is ring pitch. `bevel` is flange width. Four spokes on x/y. Five blades and a hub (`HUB` 0.22 of radius) in the cavity. Colors: paint, cavity, dirt. `wear` dirties edges, flange rust, streaks under the grille.

Height: housing ~0.55, flange raised, cavity sunk, wire at 0.42. Roughness: entry factor; cavity glossier.

Exact alignment, 1 x 1 m at 1024 px.
