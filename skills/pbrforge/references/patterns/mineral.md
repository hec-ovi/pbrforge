# mineral

Continuous paving body. File: `src/gen/pattern/MineralFinish.ts`. Recipe: `batch/cyberpunk/street-construction.json` (`street-paving-body`).

Noise at physical scales: aggregate 6-11 mm, binder ~55 mm, pores ~9 mm. Optional `localized-damp` response on the spec. `wear` above 0.5 adds sparse fractures. One face color. No joints.

Used for tiled opaque mineral, not photos.
